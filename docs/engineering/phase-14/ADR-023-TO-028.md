# Phase 14: Personalization & Recommendation Intelligence Engine
## Architectural Decision Records (ADR-023 through ADR-028)

**Phase:** Phase 14 — Personalization & Recommendation Intelligence  
**Status:** `APPROVED & IMPLEMENTED`  
**Authority:** Principal AI Systems Architect, Ayurvedic Knowledge Systems Engineer, Safety Engineer  

---

### ADR-023: Deterministic Rule Engine vs. Generative/LLM Recommendation Generation

- **Status:** APPROVED
- **Context:** Recommendation generation in healthcare and wellness carries severe hallucination and liability risks. LLM-generated recommendations can fabricate ingredient synergies, invent medical cure promises, or bypass non-diagnostic boundaries.
- **Decision:** Implement Phase 14 recommendations strictly through a deterministic, auditable rule engine (`evaluateRecommendationRules`). Every recommendation item must be explicitly defined, mapped to classical Shastric principles, and triggered by verified input signals. LLMs are prohibited from autonomously inventing ingredients, formulations, or medical claims.
- **Consequences:** 100% reproducible evaluations, zero hallucination of herbs or contraindications, and complete decision traces for forensic auditing.

---

### ADR-024: Evidence Eligibility Gating Prior to Recommendation

- **Status:** APPROVED
- **Context:** Presenting recommendations to users when upstream evidence is absent, conflicting, or unassessed creates false certainty and medical liability.
- **Decision:** Implement a strict pre-recommendation gate (`evaluateEvidenceEligibility`). If the upstream fusion result is missing, if evidence strength is `NONE`, or if material conflicts exist between modalities, recommendation generation is blocked. The system returns `BASELINE_ONLY` guidance with an explicit refusal rationale.
- **Consequences:** Prevents ungrounded advice. Preserves trust boundaries established in Phases 10–13.

---

### ADR-025: Confidence Ceiling Inheritance from Multimodal Fusion

- **Status:** APPROVED
- **Context:** A recommendation engine should never claim higher confidence than the empirical observation and evidence that justified it.
- **Decision:** Hardcode a confidence ceiling map (`CONFIDENCE_CEILING_MAP`) binding `EvidenceStrength` directly to `RecommendationConfidence`:
  - `NONE` → `BASELINE_ONLY`
  - `LOW` → `LOW_CONFIDENCE`
  - `MODERATE` → `MODERATE_CONFIDENCE`
  - `HIGH` → `HIGH_CONFIDENCE`
  Under no circumstances can a rule assign higher confidence than the upstream fusion layer achieved.
- **Consequences:** Downstream advice is always epistemically honest and proportional to sensory quality.

### ADR-026: Mandatory Patch-Test Safety Invariant for Topical Botanicals

- **Status:** APPROVED
- **Context:** Individual biological sensitivities, contact allergies, and barrier reactivity can occur independently of constitutional Prakriti or general herbal safety.
- **Decision:** Enforce that every topical botanical recommendation item and formulation must carry `patchTestRequired = true` and actionable 24-hour patch test instructions (`Twak Pariksha Vidhi`). Additionally, `P14-RULE-002` (Universal Patch Test Safety Notice) always fires across all evaluations as an unskippable supplementary directive.
- **Consequences:** Eliminates negligence risk regarding adverse topical reactions.

---

### ADR-027: User-Governed Exclusions and Preference Primacy

- **Status:** APPROVED
- **Context:** Users may have known allergies (e.g. sesame allergy, sandalwood sensitivity) or ethical/lifestyle preferences that must override algorithmic recommendations.
- **Decision:** The rule engine inspects `userPreferences.excludedIngredients` against both botanical IDs, English names, and Sanskrit names. If a match occurs, the formulation is blocked, `rulesExcluded` is incremented, and a decision trace entry records `BLOCKED_BY_USER_EXCLUSION` with the exact matched ingredient.
- **Consequences:** Respects user autonomy and prevents allergen exposure.

---

### ADR-028: Audit Traceability and Decision Explainability

- **Status:** APPROVED
- **Context:** Black-box personalization prevents clinical oversight and adversarial auditing.
- **Decision:** Every personalization evaluation outputs a structured `PersonalizationAuditTrace` containing the evaluation ID, timestamp, eligibility gate output, count of evaluated/fired/skipped/excluded rules, individual `RecommendationDecisionTrace` records, and applied ceilings.
- **Consequences:** Fully inspectable by clinical reviewers, compliance officers, and automated adversarial test suites.
