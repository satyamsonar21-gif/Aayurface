### 1. Longitudinal Intelligence Principles

1. **Non-Clinical Boundary Invariant:** Longitudinal tracking monitors cosmetic wellness characteristics and self-reported consistency. It NEVER claims to track dermatological disease remission, pathological recovery, or clinical efficacy.
2. **Derived Metric Tracking:** In alignment with biometric data minimization (`DEC-004`), longitudinal progress tracks derived numerical vectors (`VisualObservations`), eliminating the necessity of storing high-resolution facial images over time.
3. **Multi-Signal Delta Analysis:** Trends compare multi-modal dimensions: objective visual indices, subjective user wellness logs, and daily routine adherence rates.

#### Explicit Separation of Tracked Dimensions:
The system tracks five distinct, transparently defined dimensions:

| Dimension ID | Tracked Metric Category | Underlying Objective Data | Scientific Meaning | What It Does NOT Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DIM-01** | **Observed Visual Signals** | CIELAB $a^*$ redness mean, GLCM surface texture entropy, melanin uniformity variance. | Relative shift in skin surface reflection, redness balance, or roughness indices over time. | Does NOT measure medical inflammation, rosacea cure, or pathological healing. |
| **DIM-02** | **User-Reported Symptoms** | Hydration rating (1–5), perceived skin comfort, sleep quality hours. | Subjective self-assessment of daily comfort and environmental stress. | Does NOT represent objective clinical vitals. |
| **DIM-03** | **Daily Routine Adherence** | Percentage of scheduled Morning/Evening rituals checked in `routine_tracking`. | User discipline and consistency in applying balancing wellness habits. | Does NOT guarantee pharmacological efficacy. |
| **DIM-04** | **System Confidence Shifts** | Calibrated Confidence score ($Q_{cap} \times C_{input} \times A \times 100\%$). | Stability of data inputs and alignment across modalities across scans. | Does NOT indicate personal wellness health score. |
| **DIM-05** | **Doshic Balance Tendency** | Fused constitutional percentages (Vata%, Pitta%, Kapha%). | Dynamic seasonal shift in dominant physiological and elemental tendencies. | Does NOT represent permanent genetic constitutional alteration. |

### 3. Delta Calculation Engine & Data Architecture

Longitudinal checkpoints aggregate baseline vs. current metrics:

```typescript
export interface LongitudinalCheckpoint {
  userId: string;
  baselineScanId: string;
  currentScanId: string;
  daysActive: number; // e.g., 30
  visualDeltas: {
    rednessIndexDelta: number;    // Normalized delta: -0.15 (15% reduction in surface warmth signal)
    textureRoughnessDelta: number; // Normalized delta: -0.10 (10% smoother surface reflection)
    melaninUniformityDelta: number; // Normalized delta: +0.05
  };
  routineAdherenceRate: number;   // e.g., 0.82 (82% of days rituals were completed)
  dominantDoshicTrend: 'STABILIZING' | 'AGGRAVATED' | 'BALANCED';
  trendInterpretationText: string;
  generatedAt: string;
}
```

#### Trend Interpretation Guardrail:
Trend summaries generated by the AI layer are strictly validated against a non-medical safety schema:
* *Permitted Language:* *"Your skin surface redness signal shows a 12% cooling trend over the past 30 days, correlating with high adherence to your cooling Pitta evening routine."*
* *Prohibited Language:* *"Your acne has healed by 12%"* or *"Your dermatitis is cured."*
