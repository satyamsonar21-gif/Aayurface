### 1. Classification Vocabulary

* **CONFIRMED:** Explicitly decided by project owner or directly supported by existing repository/product requirements.
* **PROPOSED:** Engineering/product recommendation that has not yet been formally approved.
* **OPEN DECISION:** Requires explicit stakeholder resolution before architectural finalization or implementation.
* **UNVERIFIED:** Claim or parameter exists, but empirical evidence is currently insufficient to validate it.
* **RESEARCH-INFORMED:** Supported by research literature or strategic direction, but not yet empirically validated in AayurFace.
